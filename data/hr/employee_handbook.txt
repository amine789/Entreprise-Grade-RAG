PTO and Vacation Policy
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued
monthly at a rate of 1.25 days per month. PTO can be used for vacation, personal time,
or illness. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year;
any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st. Employees must submit PTO
requests through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance, except in cases
of emergency or illness. Managers approve or deny requests within 2 business days.

Leave of Absence Policy
Employees who need extended time off beyond standard PTO -- for medical, parental, or
family care reasons -- may apply for a leave of absence. Parental leave provides 12
weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver,
following the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child. Medical leave follows
applicable federal and state law (e.g. FMLA in the United States) and requires
supporting documentation from a healthcare provider. Leave requests should be
submitted to HR at least 30 days in advance when the need is foreseeable.

Benefits Enrollment
New employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll in health, dental, and
vision insurance plans through the benefits portal. Outside of this initial window,
enrollment changes can only be made during the annual open enrollment period each
November, or after a qualifying life event (marriage, birth of a child, loss of other
coverage). The company covers 80% of the employee's health insurance premium and 50%
of premiums for dependents. Employees are also automatically enrolled in a 401(k)
retirement plan with a 4% company match, vesting immediately.

Payroll Schedule
Employees are paid on a bi-weekly basis, with paychecks issued every other Friday.
Direct deposit is required for all employees and can be set up through the payroll
portal. Pay stubs, including tax withholding and deduction details, are available
online at all times. Any payroll discrepancies should be reported to HR within 5
business days of the affected pay date so they can be corrected in the next cycle.

Expense Reimbursement Policy
Employees may submit expense reports for approved business expenses, including
travel, client meals, and work-related software or equipment purchases. Reports must
be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred, along with itemized
receipts. Expenses over $500 require prior manager approval before being incurred.
Reimbursements are processed within 2 pay cycles of report approval and are paid out
via direct deposit alongside regular payroll.

Onboarding Process
New employees complete onboarding during their first week, which includes account and
equipment setup, benefits enrollment, and required compliance training (security
awareness, code of conduct, and anti-harassment training). Each new hire is assigned
a manager-designated onboarding buddy for their first 30 days to help them navigate
team processes and company tools. A 30-60-90 day check-in schedule is used to track
ramp-up progress with the employee's manager.

Performance Review Process
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December. Each
review includes a self-assessment, manager feedback, and peer feedback from at least
two colleagues selected by the employee and manager together. Ratings use a 4-point
scale (Does Not Meet, Meets, Exceeds, Significantly Exceeds Expectations) and directly
inform annual merit increases and bonus eligibility. Employees on a performance
improvement plan (PIP) are reviewed on a 30-day cycle instead of the standard
bi-annual schedule until the plan concludes.
